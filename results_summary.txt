Project Title:
Transcriptomic Analysis of Carboplatin Resistance in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

Dataset:
GSE309617

Analysis Performed:
- Differential gene expression analysis using DESeq2
- Volcano plot generation
- Heatmap visualization
- Significant DEG extraction

Key Results:
- Resistant and sensitive TNBC tumors showed distinct transcriptomic patterns.
- Significant DEGs associated with carboplatin resistance were identified.
- Heatmap clustering demonstrated transcriptomic separation between resistant and sensitive tumors.
- Volcano plot revealed multiple significantly altered genes.

Biological Interpretation:
The analysis suggests that carboplatin resistance in TNBC is associated with substantial transcriptomic remodeling involving pathways linked to tumor progression, survival signaling, and therapeutic adaptation.

Potential Future Analyses:
- KEGG pathway enrichment
- GO Biological Process enrichment
- Hub gene network analysis
- Epigenetic pathway interpretation
- Integration with DNA methylation datasets
